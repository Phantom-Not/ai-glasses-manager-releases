Camera filters · Added in 2.6.3

Give your captures a different look.

Capture familiar places in a more personal way. Try Matrix, Cyber Glitch or Prism in Manager’s Camera, and explore the photo examples below.

How to use the filters

  1. Open Camera in Manager, or use the Manager Camera shortcut.
  2. Open the filter picker and choose the look you want.
  3. Close the filter picker to return to the camera preview.
  4. Press Manager’s shutter button to take your photo.

Matrix

Give the scene a Matrix-inspired look with green code across your view.

Cyber Glitch

Break familiar shapes into colourful blocks and digital distortion.

Prism

Bend and swirl the scene for a more surreal view.

Open any photo to see the full image.

01

Three named styles, in one Camera

Version 2.6.3 introduced Matrix, Cyber Glitch and Prism. Choose the style in Camera rather than moving to a separate editing app.

The look of a filter changes with the scene and lighting. Try a few different subjects to see how each style suits your captures.

02

Photo and video controls

Switch capture modes, use the viewfinder, adjust zoom and framing, and turn grid controls on or off. Camera settings include shutter and recording sound controls.

Visual feedback confirms when a capture succeeds or fails.

03

Your captures stay close

Open Album to browse photos and videos, view pictures, play video and delete media you no longer want. Manager’s Gallery is designed for the glasses display.

Optional Manager Shortcuts let you open Camera or Album directly from the Rokid launcher.
