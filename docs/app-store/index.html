The built-in App Store

A bigger app catalogue. On your glasses.

Explore Android apps alongside the projects people are building specifically for Rokid.

01

A broad Android catalogue

Manager’s App Store brings together curated, developer, global and China-friendly selections. It is useful for finding ordinary Android apps as well as specialist glasses tools.

Search by name, browse categories and read an app’s details before deciding what to install. The catalogue contains thousands of listings; the exact selection changes with available sources.

  • Browse apps, utilities and media tools.
  • Explore global and China-friendly selections.
  • See app details and available download options.
02

A separate home for Rokid developers

The curated developer collection highlights community projects made for Rokid Glasses. It is one part of the App Store, alongside the wider Android catalogue.

A community listing does not mean Manager owns or develops that app. Each project remains the work of its own publisher.

03

Stay in control of downloads

Manage downloads with pause, resume and cancel controls. Downloads can continue in the background while you leave the Store. When a supported APK is ready, you can open the installation flow on the glasses.

  • Pause a download and resume it later.
  • Cancel a download you no longer need.
  • Install a supported APK when the download is ready.
  • See available updates for supported installed apps.
04

Keep browsing what is already loaded

Previously loaded app information remains available when the connection drops. Manager also provides offline and network guidance so a missing connection is easier to understand.

Sources and download options can change. Some listings use an external store, and Android app features may depend on hardware or input methods the glasses do not have.
