The complete feature guide

What Manager can do.

A practical reference for the current public release, 2.6.3. Each section describes a tool you can use on the glasses.

App Store and installed appsCamera and three filtersGallery and media viewingFile Explorer and Wi-Fi DropDevice settings and inputSound and swipe feedbackCleaner and Live IMUOptional AI and voice controlsManager ShortcutsInterface, languages and updates
01

App Store and installed apps

Browse thousands of Android app listings across curated, developer, global and China-friendly selections. Search, view app details, manage downloads and install supported APKs.

  • Pause, resume or cancel downloads.
  • Let downloads continue in the background.
  • See available updates for supported installed apps.
  • Browse previously loaded information when offline.
02

Camera and three filters

Capture photos and video from Manager’s Camera. Choose from Matrix, Cyber Glitch and Prism, introduced in 2.6.3.

  • Adjust mode, framing and zoom.
  • Show or hide grid controls.
  • Control shutter and recording sounds.
  • See capture success or failure feedback.
03

Gallery and media viewing

Browse photo and video categories in Album. View pictures, play saved video and delete unwanted media directly on the glasses.

Gallery refreshes its media index when opened. Its viewing controls are designed for the glasses display.

04

File Explorer and Wi-Fi Drop

Browse local folders and open common files. Transfer files between the glasses and supported phones or computers using Wi-Fi Drop’s browser interface.

Upload APKs with Wi-Fi Drop, then open them in File Explorer for installation.

05

Device settings and input

Reach Rokid and Android settings through one consistent menu. Open Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, keyboard, installed-app, storage and battery tools.

Available system actions depend on the glasses firmware and Android version.

06

Sound and swipe feedback

Adjust system volume and glasses volume separately, or keep glasses volume at a chosen level with the lock control. Configure swipe feedback from Manager.

07

Cleaner and Live IMU

Use Cleaner for one-tap maintenance or to review running apps.

Live IMU shows compass, accelerometer, gyroscope, pitch and roll information. You can calibrate or zero readings when working with motion and orientation.

08

Optional AI and voice controls

Show the optional AI microphone control from Manager Settings when you want hands-free access. The built-in command guide provides supported English and Chinese examples.

You can hide the AI control from Home when you do not need it. Interface languages and voice-command support are different things.

09

Manager Shortcuts

Add Camera, Album, App Store and File Explorer as optional shortcuts in the Rokid launcher. Each shortcut opens its Manager tool directly.

Enable or remove these shortcuts from Manager Settings.

10

Interface, languages and updates

AR Mode, visible focus states and consistent layouts help make the tools usable on the glasses. Manager can check its own future releases through Manager Settings > App Update.

The public 2.6.3 interface supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Cantonese (Hong Kong), Japanese, Korean, Russian, French, German and Spanish.
