# AI Glasses Manager 2.6.4

For Rokid Glasses. Independently developed by Bruce. Formerly Rokid Manager.

## English

### Camera

- Added VHS Camcorder, Temporal Lights, and Predator Vision for photos and videos.
- Improved photo processing, capture reliability, and zoom. Added low-battery protection.
- Use the glasses’ dedicated camera button to take a photo or start and stop recording.
- Camera Settings includes Tap and Swipe zoom, gridlines, and aspect ratio.

### Manager Settings

- Choose Manager’s language independently of the glasses’ system language. Arabic joins ten existing languages.

<img src="https://phantom-not.github.io/ai-glasses-manager-releases/images/manager-language-picker.png" alt="Manager 2.6.4 language picker / 管理器语言选择" width="240">

### App Store

- Updated the catalog and improved keyboard and mouse navigation.

### Wi-Fi Drop

- Transfers can continue while the display sleeps. You have 10 minutes to start a transfer, with extra time for transfers already underway.

## 中文

### 相机

- 新增 VHS 复古摄像机、流光轨迹和铁血战士视觉滤镜，拍照和录像均可使用。
- 改进照片处理、拍摄稳定性和变焦流畅度，新增低电量保护。
- 按下眼镜上的独立相机键即可拍照，或开始和停止录像。
- 可在相机设置中开启「点击后滑动变焦」，也可设置网格线和画面比例。

### 管理器设置

- 可单独选择管理器的显示语言，无需更改眼镜的系统语言。新增阿拉伯语，现共支持 11 种语言。

### 应用商店

- 更新应用目录，并改进键盘和鼠标导航。

### Wi-Fi 传输

- 眼镜熄屏后仍可继续传输文件。可在 10 分钟内开始传输，已开始的传输可在倒计时结束后继续一小段时间。
